# DRONE SYPHON

Working title (keep the Y). Repo: `drone-syphon`.
2D side expedition. You ride until the pack dies. You send the quad to steal watts. You ride farther than you should.

GRIDRUNNER stays on `grok`. This is a new game.

## Fantasy

A light motorcycle and a harvest drone on a dead service corridor. Power is the only clock.

## v0 slice

One strip. Road, one crate, one live panel, a gate that needs charge.

- `A/D` move
- `F` bike ↔ drone
- `E` drink watts on a source (drone)
- `Space` hop

Win: trailer ≥ 18, east gate opens.

## Physics

See in-game. Bike pack dies with speed. Drone has a 42 m tether and a hover battery. Syphon is overlap + hold E on the panel.

## Pixel art

480×270, nearest neighbor, 8-color dust/lime/rust palette.
